Minecraft hosting with UPI payment: a practical ordering guide

Order TreeMC Minecraft hosting with UPI through Cashfree. Check the plan, Paymenter invoice, payment state and server access before starting your world.

For a concrete final-price example, Windows RDP hosting starts at ₹685/month with a Windows Server licence and same-price renewals; GST is not charged.

The useful part of UPI checkout is knowing exactly which server you are buying and which invoice the payment belongs to. Here is the path from choosing a Minecraft plan to checking your server access, without confusing a cart click with a paid order.

The short answer

Choose the matching billing product, review its monthly invoice, then pay through Cashfree. Keep the invoice reference until payment and access are confirmed.

Choose a plan your server can use

The cheap Minecraft hosting in India page lists Intel 1 GB at ₹20/month and Intel 2 GB at ₹40/month, with larger Intel, EPYC and Ryzen allocations above them. The smallest plan is an entry allocation, not a promise that every modern release or modpack fits.

Write down the edition, release, plugins or server pack before selecting RAM. Read the full CPU and storage allocation on the card. The listed direct billing plans use Mumbai; ask about Noida stock before ordering for that city.

Review the website cart

Select Add to Cart, remove any plans you added only for comparison, and review the service terms yourself before checking the agreement box. The cart can contain several services, but a link to one Paymenter product does not buy every item at once.

The Minecraft order buttons lead to the matching processor category and RAM tier on panel.treemchost.pro. Check that the name, resources and monthly amount still match the plan you chose. Never send a password or card details in a public Discord message.

Use the Cashfree option on the invoice

Cashfree supports UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. Use the available method shown for your invoice and review its amount before authorizing payment. A promotional code, if accepted by billing, should be reflected in the invoice you actually pay.

If your bank shows a debit while the invoice remains pending, keep the invoice number and gateway reference and contact billing support. Do not assume a failed browser redirect means the payment failed, or pay repeatedly just to refresh the page.

Confirm payment and the server details

An Add to Cart toast or a return to the website is not payment confirmation. Check the paid invoice and the issued service details in the billing account. Keep a copy of the order’s selected location and allocation.

Read the 24-hour refund policy before paying: its window starts at deployment and eligibility conditions and deductions apply. If access or allocation differs from the order, raise the issue promptly through a private billing ticket.

Prepare your first world

Use the supplied panel login, check the Java requirement for your release, and install only compatible plugins or a matching modpack. Every paid Minecraft plan has three backup slots; create a recovery point before imports, updates or plugin changes.

For native Bedrock, order the Java plan and open a Discord ticket to request the switch. For cross-play, follow the Geyser setup guide. Discord and email support are available 24/7 without a guaranteed reply time.

Before you finish

  • Match the selected product, allocation and location.
  • Review the invoice, terms and private support route.
  • Check the delivered access and keep a recovery copy.
