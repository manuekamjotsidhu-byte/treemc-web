TreeMC Host ("Company", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data in a transparent and secure manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access our website (https://treemchost.pro), client portal, control panels, servers, APIs, and related services. By registering an account or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors of our website, registered customers, users of free and paid hosting services, affiliates and partners, and individuals contacting us for support. This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services not owned or controlled by TreeMC Host.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide secure, reliable hosting services: (2.1 Directly Provided) Full legal name, email address, phone number, billing address, company name (if applicable), government ID (if required for verification), payment details, support tickets, and Discord communications. (2.2 Automatically Collected) IP address, login timestamps, browser type and version, device type, OS, referring URLs, pages visited, bandwidth usage, and server access logs. (2.3 Service-Related) CPU, RAM, storage, network logs, abuse activity logs, and firewall alerts. We do not monitor private content unless required for abuse investigation or legal compliance.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on: Performance of a contract (providing hosting services), legal obligations (tax, fraud prevention, law enforcement requests), legitimate business interests (security, system stability, fraud detection), and your consent where required by law.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: Create and manage your account, process payments and invoices, deliver hosting services, provide technical support, detect and prevent fraud or abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, maintain infrastructure stability, send important service notifications, and improve platform performance. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

5. Payment Processing

All payments are processed through trusted third-party providers such as Stripe and PayPal. TreeMC Host does not store complete credit card numbers on its servers. Payment providers handle sensitive financial information under their own privacy and security standards.

6. Data Retention Policy

We retain data only as long as necessary: Active accounts retain data while services remain active; Inactive accounts may be deleted after extended inactivity; Billing records are retained as required by tax laws; Abuse and fraud records may be retained indefinitely for security purposes; Suspended accounts are retained to prevent repeat violations. After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited data with payment processors, infrastructure providers, control panel vendors, legal authorities when required by law, and professional advisors. We only share information necessary for service delivery or legal compliance. We do not share personal data for advertising resale.

8. Law Enforcement Requests

We may disclose information if required by court orders, government authorities, law enforcement investigations, or legal obligations under applicable law. We reserve the right to comply with valid legal requests.

9. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices including SSL/TLS encryption, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, access control restrictions, role-based staff permissions, security monitoring, and DDoS protection systems. Despite these measures, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for securing your login credentials, enabling strong passwords, maintaining backups of your hosted data, keeping your registered email active and monitored, and implementing security measures on VPS servers. TreeMC Host is not responsible for account compromise caused by weak passwords or user negligence.

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to maintain login sessions, store user preferences, improve website performance, and analyze traffic patterns. You may disable cookies via browser settings; however, certain features may not function correctly.

12. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored or processed in data centers located outside your country of residence (including India, Germany, Singapore, US, and Canada). By using our services, you consent to such international transfers. We take reasonable steps to ensure data protection regardless of location.

13. Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, or request portability of your personal data. Requests must be submitted via the official support ticket system. We may verify identity before fulfilling requests.

14. Account Deletion

16. Children's Privacy
