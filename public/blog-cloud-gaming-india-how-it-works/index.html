Cloud Gaming in India: How It Works and What You Need

By TreeMC · Updated 15 November 2026

Cloud gaming in India lets you play PC games on a powerful remote computer, using your own phone, laptop or low-end PC as the screen. You pay by the hour instead of buying an expensive graphics card.

How it works

You book a gaming rig, connect to it, and the game runs on the remote GPU. Video and sound stream to your device, and your controls go back.

What you need

  • A stable internet connection (wired or good Wi-Fi)
  • Low ping to the rig, see how to read ping
  • A device to connect from

GPU options at TreeMC

Book on our cloud gaming page. Some device types may be out of stock; the page shows what is available.

Who it suits

Players with weak PCs, people who want to try heavy games before buying hardware, and friends who want to play together.

Tips

  1. Test your ping first
  2. Close downloads while playing
  3. Use a wired connection if possible
