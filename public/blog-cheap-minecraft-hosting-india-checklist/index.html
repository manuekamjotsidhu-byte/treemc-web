Minecraft Server Costs: A Checklist for SMP Owners

Budget for your Minecraft SMP by checking renewal costs, RAM, backups and migration before choosing an Indian host.

By TreeMC · 4 October 2026 · Updated 17 November 2026 · 2 min read

Keeping an SMP affordable makes sense, especially when a few friends are sharing the bill. The trick is finding a plan that runs your setup comfortably instead of choosing the smallest number on the pricing page and hoping for the best.

Work out what you are buying

Write down your Minecraft release, server software and plugins or modpack. Then check the available CPU allocation, RAM and storage. Two plans with the same RAM can still offer different resources. If you already have a server, bring its busy-session measurements to the comparison.

Turn the advertised rate into a real bill

TreeMC's published Intel Minecraft range starts at ₹20 per GB per month. In that catalog, 2GB is ₹40 per month and 6GB is ₹120. The AMD and Ryzen families have different rates. Check the selected billing product for the current total and period before paying.

Don't assume the starting price includes every extra you might need. Ask about backup arrangements, storage limits and any setup assistance relevant to your project.

Make migration and recovery part of the decision

Before moving an existing world, confirm file access, supported software and enough storage for the live files. Decide where your independent backups will go. A low monthly bill isn't helpful if the plan can't accommodate the server you actually intend to move.

Choose affordability you can keep using

People searching for the most affordable hosting in India often mean the lowest cost that still does the job. That's a better target than an isolated entry price. Start with a suitable plan, keep your plugins tidy and review real usage before upgrading.

If you're unsure, send support your version, pack name and expected activity. A specific question gets you a more useful answer than asking whether a plan is good for Minecraft in general.

Your next steps

  • Describe your software and workload.
  • Compare the full monthly bill.
  • Check storage, access and recovery plans.

Questions readers ask

Is the cheapest plan always the best value?

Only if it meets your requirements. A plan that can't run your software comfortably can cost you time and an early migration.
