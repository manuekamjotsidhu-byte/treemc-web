An RDP price is useful only when you know what is inside it. A remote desktop offer may differ in operating system, memory, disk, network port, and DDoS mitigation. Here is how to read TreeMC’s seven-plan Noida Windows RDP catalog clearly.

What ₹685 per month includes

TreeMC’s Windows RDP plans in India start with India - RDP XS at ₹685/month in Noida, India. It includes 8 GB DDR4 ECC RAM, 4 Intel Xeon vCores, 80 GB NVMe SSD storage, Unmetered (1 Gbps Port), Advanced DDoS Mitigation, and full administrator Remote Desktop access.

All seven listed RDP plans include Unmetered bandwidth on a 1 Gbps Port and Advanced DDoS Mitigation.

How the seven plans compare

India - RDP S is ₹959/month for 12 GB DDR4 ECC RAM, 4 Intel Xeon vCores, and 100 GB NVMe SSD. India - RDP M is ₹1,370/month with 16 GB RAM, 6 vCores, and 150 GB NVMe. India - RDP L is ₹1,918/month with 24 GB RAM, 8 vCores, and 200 GB NVMe. India - RDP XL is ₹2,467/month with 32 GB RAM, 10 vCores, and 300 GB NVMe. India - RDP XXL is ₹3,014/month with 48 GB RAM, 12 vCores, and 350 GB NVMe. India - RDP 2XL is ₹3,068/month with 64 GB RAM, 16 vCores, and 400 GB NVMe.

Use unit prices carefully

Compare RAM, vCore count, and NVMe SSD size together against your target software. Windows uses part of the allocated storage, so leave headroom for Windows updates, application installs, and logs.

Check Windows compatibility before ordering

India - RDP plans come with Windows Server 2022 ready out of the box, and other editions can be requested on Discord. If your application runs natively on Linux without a desktop GUI, compare our Cloud VPS catalog separately.

Order on Discord and verify your desktop

Ask our team on Discord to confirm your Windows edition and deploy your Noida RDP server. After login, change credentials as instructed and confirm your installed RAM, disk, and network route.

Budget for the whole workflow
