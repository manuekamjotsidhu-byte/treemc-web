VPS vs VDS: Which One Should You Choose?

Both a VPS and a VDS are virtual servers with your own operating system and root access. The difference is how the CPU is allocated.

VPS (virtual private server)

A VPS gives you a guaranteed slice of a physical machine. It is the affordable choice for websites, bots, small game servers and apps. At TreeMC, Cloud VPS plans start at ₹359 per month.

VDS (virtual dedicated server)

A VDS gives you CPU cores reserved only for you, so performance stays steady even when the host is busy. It suits heavy game servers, databases and large communities. At TreeMC, India VDS plans start at ₹1,699 per month (Intel Xeon) and ₹1,999 per month (AMD EPYC) with 6 to 12 dedicated vCores and up to 64 GB DDR4 ECC RAM.
