How to Host a Minecraft Server in India (Step by Step, 2026)

By TreeMC · Updated 15 November 2026

You can host a Minecraft server in India in two ways: on your own PC, or with a hosting plan. Hosting at home is free but your PC must stay on and your internet must handle it. A hosting plan stays online 24/7. Here is the simple path.

Step 1: Choose Java or Bedrock

Java is for PC players and has the most plugins and mods. Bedrock is for phones, consoles and Windows. You can let both join with Geyser, see Geyser and Floodgate crossplay.

Step 2: Pick a plan

For friends and a small survival world, Budget is enough. For plugins and 20 or more players, choose Performance. For modpacks and communities, choose Max Performance. TreeMC plans start at ₹20 per month on our Minecraft page.

Step 3: Order and get your details

Press Order on the plan, choose Discord or email, and our team confirms and sends payment details (UPI in India). After confirmation you get your server address.

Step 4: Set it up

  1. Open the control panel and start the server
  2. Set your options, see server.properties explained
  3. Make yourself operator, see how to give yourself OP
  4. Add plugins or mods, see how to install plugins

Step 5: Invite friends

Share the server address. Java players add it under Multiplayer. Read how to join a Minecraft server.

Hosting at home instead

You need to open a port on your router and keep your PC on. See how to port forward. Many home connections in India use shared addresses (CGNAT) that block this, which is another reason people choose a hosting plan.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a Minecraft server in India?

Choose a hosting plan or host at home, pick Java or Bedrock, set up the server, and share the address with friends.

How much RAM do I need?

Small friend servers run well on a few GB. Plugin and modded servers need more. See our RAM guides.

Can I host Minecraft for free?

Yes, on your own PC or with free hosts, but they have limits. Paid hosting stays online 24/7.
