Bandwidth, uplink and transfer: reading a VPS plan

Understand the difference between network speed and data-transfer allowance, and ask the right questions before choosing a VPS.

By TreeMC · 4 October 2026 · 2 min read

The RDP plans in Mumbai list 90,000 GB monthly transfer on a 2 Gbps uplink. Compare transfer allowance and uplink capacity separately.

The plan lists a fast uplink. Does that mean you can transfer as much data as you like? Not necessarily. Speed and monthly allowance answer different questions, and you need both answers before hosting something that moves a lot of data.

Separate rate from quantity

An uplink figure such as Gbps describes a rate. A monthly transfer figure such as TB describes an amount of data. Neither tells the whole story on its own. Confirm whether the advertised rate is shared, capped or subject to other limits.

Think about your traffic pattern

A small API, a file-download service and a game server can create very different traffic. Estimate how users interact with your application instead of choosing by page views alone. Backups and transfers between systems may also matter to your overall network use.

Ask what happens at the limit

Confirm how traffic is counted and what happens if you exceed an allowance: extra charges, reduced speed or another policy. Read acceptable-use restrictions for your intended workload. Do not interpret a short marketing label as permission for every type of traffic.

Monitor after launch

Record normal traffic and investigate unexpected increases. A sudden rise may come from a legitimate event, a misconfigured job or abuse. Use what you learn to choose future capacity, and keep copies of the terms that apply to your order.

Your next steps

  • Find both speed and allowance.
  • Check counting and overage terms.
  • Monitor real traffic after launch.

Questions readers ask

Does a 2 Gbps uplink mean unlimited transfer?

No. Uplink speed and any transfer allowance are separate details. Confirm both in the product terms.
