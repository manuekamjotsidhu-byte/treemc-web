Windows RDP in India with UPI: order and first-login checks

Buying an RDP server should leave you with a clear invoice, the right Windows version and working connection details. Use this checklist when paying with UPI so the plan, payment and desktop access all refer to the same service.

Start with the application, not the smallest price

The cheap RDP hosting in India catalog starts at ₹685/month for RDP - XS: 8 GB DDR4 ECC RAM, 4 Intel Xeon vCores, 80 GB NVMe, administrator access and a dedicated IPv4 address. Windows Server 2022 and its Windows Server licence are included.

Check that your application supports Windows Server 2022. Windows 10 and Windows 11 are not selectable in the current RDP checkout. Allow separately for application licences and any additional access licensing required by your use; an included server licence is not every software licence.

Check the final invoice before paying

Choose the matching XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL or 2XL product in Paymenter. The monthly prices shown on the RDP page are the final listed hosting prices, include the Windows Server licence and remain the same at monthly renewal. TreeMC does not charge GST on these plans.

Compare the invoice with the plan you selected. Do not treat one product link as a purchase of several services in the website cart. If the product or amount is wrong, resolve it before authorizing the payment.

Pay through Cashfree UPI

Choose an available UPI method in Cashfree for the invoice. Cards, net banking and wallets are also supported. Follow the bank or payment app’s own authorization flow; never share a UPI PIN, OTP or password with support.

Keep the invoice number and payment reference. If the bank reports a debit but billing remains pending, contact billing support with those references privately. A browser closing or a slow redirect is not sufficient evidence that another payment is needed.

Use the issued desktop details

TreeMC confirms instant delivery after successful payment for its RDP plans. Check the paid invoice and the connection information issued for that service. If a paid order has not supplied access, open a billing ticket rather than trying a public test address as the login.

Confirm Mumbai or Noida availability for your exact order. Connect with a compatible Remote Desktop client and keep the supplied address, account and credentials private. Verify unexpected certificate or identity warnings with support.

Check resources and keep recovery access

After connecting, use Windows System Information and Task Manager to check the operating system, memory and processor allocation. Disk capacity includes space used by Windows, updates and installed applications. Try your normal application before moving important work.

The RDP allowance is 90,000 GB monthly transfer on a 2 Gbps uplink; the port speed does not guarantee the rate of your home connection. Keep backups and review the 24-hour refund terms promptly if something differs from your order. Discord and email support are available 24/7 without a fixed reply-time promise.

Before you finish

  • Match the selected product, allocation and location.
  • Review the invoice, terms and private support route.
  • Check the delivered access and keep a recovery copy.
