Ask on Discord
What is TreeMC’s cheapest VPS plan in India?

The Intel 8 GB VPS costs ₹300/month with 2 vCores, 8 GB RAM, 50 GB SSD and root access. If your application needs 4 vCores or a larger disk, the Intel 16 GB plan includes 80 GB SSD for ₹800/month.

Can I choose Mumbai or Noida?

Both cities are listed for India VPS hosting. Ask billing to confirm stock for your processor, plan size and preferred city before paying.

Which plan should I choose for a Windows desktop?

For a remote Windows desktop with administrator access, compare the Windows RDP hosting plans. The ₹300 VPS listing covers a virtual server with root access. The RDP page lists Windows licensing and desktop options.

How do I order a server?

Use the product checkout for Intel. For AMD EPYC or Ryzen, sign in to billing and open a ticket with the plan name and preferred city.

Which Linux versions can I install on an Intel VPS?

All six Intel plans offer Ubuntu 22.04, Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 12 and Debian 13.0. Choose the image your application supports in the Intel VPS configuration screen. For an AMD plan, include your preferred distribution and version in your billing ticket.

Does the ₹300 listing specify IPv4 and bandwidth?

The Intel 8 GB listing specifies CPU, memory and disk. For a public-facing app, ask billing how many public IPv4 addresses you get, what monthly transfer is included and what additional usage costs in your chosen city.

When can I request a VPS refund?

If you change your mind, you can request a refund within 24 hours of deployment. Where applicable, TreeMC deducts the cost of the assigned IPv4 address. See the refund policy for eligibility and other cases.
