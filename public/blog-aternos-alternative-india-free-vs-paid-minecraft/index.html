Aternos Alternative in India: Free vs Paid Minecraft Hosting

By TreeMC · Updated 15 November 2026

Free Minecraft hosts such as Aternos are a good way to try a server with friends. But if you want your world online all the time, with plugins, backups and more players, you will probably look for an Aternos alternative. Here is an honest comparison. Terms of free services change, so check each provider's current rules.

What free hosting usually means

  • Servers often start only when you press start, and may stop when nobody is online
  • Queues can appear at busy times
  • Limits on RAM, players, plugins or mods
  • The server may be in a region far from India, which adds ping

What paid hosting gives you

  • A server that stays online 24/7
  • Choose your RAM, CPU and location
  • Full file access for plugins, mods and custom worlds
  • Backups and DDoS protection
  • Support when something breaks

Which should you pick?

Use free hosting to learn and play casually. Move to paid when you run a public server, want it online all day, or need more RAM and plugins. Our full comparison is in the guide Aternos alternative: free vs paid.

A low-cost step up

How to move your world

Frequently asked questions

Is Aternos good?

It is a popular free option for casual play. Paid hosting is better when you need 24/7 uptime and more control.
