Minecraft hosting with UPI payment in India

Order Minecraft hosting in India with UPI through Cashfree. Check the Mumbai allocation, monthly INR price, Bedrock requests and refunds.

TreeMC accepts UPI through Cashfree for Minecraft hosting orders billed in INR. Direct Minecraft checkout deploys in Mumbai. Intel 1 GB starts at ₹20/month as of 16 November 2026, with a 50% CPU allocation and 10 GB NVMe SSD. Use the order summary to verify the complete price and plan before approving payment.

Choose your edition and write down the plan

Choose a Java plan for a Java Edition server. If you want a native Bedrock server for a Bedrock world, order a Java plan and request the switch in a Discord support ticket. If you want Bedrock clients to connect to a Java server, read the Geyser setup guide so that you can check whether the intended version and configuration support your players. Make the edition decision before importing a world from another host.

Write down the processor tier, RAM, CPU allocation, storage and monthly price shown on the exact card. The Intel 2 GB plan costs ₹40/month as of 16 November 2026 and lists 100% CPU and 25 GB NVMe SSD. The Intel 1 GB plan has a smaller CPU and storage allocation as well as less memory. Check the actual resource needs of your release, plugins and mods so you can compare the complete allocation.

Direct checkout deploys in Mumbai. If your group prefers Noida, ask support whether the specific plan and hardware you want can be ordered there before you pay. You can compare public network test routes from the connections your players use. Track how the game behaves after delivery as well, since a network route test measures a different part of the experience from Minecraft tick time.

Review the INR order before opening your UPI app

Select the plan on the official TreeMC site and continue to its billing checkout. Look for the plan you intended to buy in your order summary. Check the billing period, selected options and final INR total. If the cart already contains another service, review every line. A plan card describes an allocation and its monthly price. The checkout summary is where you verify what you are paying for now.

Choose UPI when Cashfree shows the payment options. Cashfree also supports cards, net banking and wallets. Follow the payment screen presented to you and verify the amount and merchant in your UPI app before approval. Use your own app to approve the payment and keep the UPI PIN inside it. The support team can investigate an order reference without your UPI PIN, one-time code or account password.

After approval, return to the billing page and check the invoice status. Save the invoice reference. If your bank records a debit and the invoice remains pending, check both records before attempting another payment. Send billing support the invoice reference, payment time, amount and any transaction reference needed to investigate. Crop unrelated account details and balances out of screenshots.

Confirm delivery and test with your actual world

After delivery, open the game panel with the details provided for the service. Compare the processor tier and published allowances with your order. Select server software compatible with your world and use the matching Java version. Start with a blank test world when you need to verify software and permissions without touching your main world. For a move, retain the original export and make a test copy first.

Invite a few players using the intended edition. Try the areas and tasks that are important to the group, such as portals, world exploration or common plugins. Watch the console during the session, then check CPU and memory. A player reporting a delay could be seeing network problems or a slow server tick. Write down the time, what the players were doing and any relevant console message so you have something concrete to compare.

Paid Minecraft plans include three backup slots, panel and file access, NVMe storage and DDoS protection. Make a backup deliberately before adding a modpack, changing versions or importing files. Keep a separate copy of the world somewhere you control. Verify the backup contents and note the server version that produced it, so someone else can restore the same setup later.

Read the refund window and keep the invoice

TreeMC’s refund policy gives you 24 hours from deployment to request a change-of-mind refund, subject to the eligibility rules and applicable IPv4 deductions. Read the complete policy for infrastructure problems, routing issues and data-loss compensation. If your service differs from the order, record what happened and contact billing support with the invoice reference and relevant details.

For the next month, check the renewal terms in billing and decide whether the allocation still fits your workload. Your game world, invoice and UPI transaction record serve different purposes. Keep a copy of the world for migration or restoration. Keep the invoice and payment reference for billing questions. If you plan to move, test an export before the current billing period ends so your players have time to verify the new server.

If the person paying is different from the person running the server, agree on who will hold the billing account and who will manage the game panel. Record the person responsible for invoices, the next renewal date and where independent backups are stored. Share the server address and any ordinary player instructions with the group. Keep billing access and administrator credentials with the people who actually need them. That separation helps when a player leaves the community, an invoice is missed or a new moderator needs access to the game.
